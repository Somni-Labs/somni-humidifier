# V3.3 Magnetic Access Panels — Design Spec

## Summary

Two flush-mount magnetic snap-off panels on the base: a backlit logo panel on the front wall (-Y) and a plain maintenance panel on the right wall (+X). Both replace existing hex mesh areas. The SOMNI LABS branding moves from the rear wall to the front panel, where it's thinned to 1mm for LED diffusion backlighting. The USB-C power cord stays on the rear wall (+Y), opposite the logo.

## Principles

1. **Flush mount** — panels sit inside a recessed lip, hairline seam only
2. **Magnetic retention** — 4mm×2mm magnets, tool-free removal
3. **Logo as diffuser** — panel thinned to 1mm in logo areas, LED strip behind illuminates through plastic
4. **Orientation** — logo on front tells user which side faces them; cord hides in back
5. **Maintenance access** — right panel gives direct access to wire bus, pump spurs, right-column pumps

## Panel Locations

### Front Panel (-Y wall)
- **Purpose:** Decorative face (backlit logo) + secondary access
- **Replaces:** Front wall hex mesh cutout
- **What's behind it:** Wet zone and center zone interior, LED strip channel

### Right Panel (+X wall)
- **Purpose:** Primary maintenance access
- **Replaces:** Right wall hex mesh cutout
- **What's behind it:** Wire bus, pump power spurs, vertical wire riser, right-column pumps

## Panel Dimensions

Panels span the same area currently occupied by hex mesh cutouts on their respective walls. The base tapers, so panel dimensions are calculated at the panel's Z center.

### Front Panel
- **Z span:** Same as current front hex mesh (`hex_panel_z` to `hex_panel_z + hex_panel_h`)
- **Width:** ~60% of base width at panel Z center (same as hex mesh width)
- **Thickness:** 3mm (matches wall thickness), thinned to 1mm in logo areas
- **Material:** PETG (same as enclosure)

### Right Panel
- **Z span:** Same as current right hex mesh
- **Width:** ~40% of base depth at panel Z center (same as hex mesh width)
- **Thickness:** 3mm solid throughout
- **Material:** PETG (same as enclosure)

## Flush Mount Detail

### Recessed Lip (in base wall)
- Step depth: 1.5mm (into the wall, from outer surface)
- Step width: 1.5mm (inward from opening edge, all around perimeter)
- The opening in the wall is the panel size + 0.3mm clearance per side
- The lip creates a shelf the panel rests against

### Panel Edge (rabbet)
- Panel has a 1.5mm × 1.5mm rabbet on all edges
- The thin outer portion (1.5mm deep) sits in the lip recess
- The thicker inner portion (1.5mm) fills the wall opening
- Result: outer surface flush with surrounding wall, ~0.3mm hairline seam

### Cross-Section (top-down view of wall):
```
     Outer surface
     ─────────────────
     |   WALL   | 0.3mm gap
     |  (3mm)   |─────────
     |          | Panel outer (1.5mm)
     |──────────|─────────────────
     |   LIP    | Panel inner (1.5mm)
     |  recess  |
     |──────────|─────────────────
     |   WALL   |
     ─────────────────
     Inner surface
```

## Magnet Mounting

### Specs
| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Magnet diameter | 4mm |
| Magnet height | 2mm |
| Count per panel | 4 (one near each corner) |
| Total new magnets | 8 |
| Pocket depth | 2.2mm (magnet + 0.2mm glue clearance) |
| Pocket diameter | 4.4mm (4mm + 0.2mm tolerance per side) |

### Magnet Pocket Locations
- 4 pockets in the lip face (facing outward, embedded in the recess shelf)
- 4 matching pockets in the panel inner face (facing inward)
- Inset ~8mm from panel corners
- Magnets attract each other to hold panel in place

### Polarity
- All lip-side magnets: same pole facing out (e.g., N)
- All panel-side magnets: opposite pole facing out (e.g., S)
- Panels are NOT interchangeable (front panel only fits front, right panel only fits right) — different sizes

## Front Panel — Backlit Logo

### Logo Layout
- **S-curve icon:** Left side of panel, vertically centered
- **"SOMNI" wordmark:** Right of icon, upper
- **"LABS" sub-text:** Right of icon, lower
- Same design as current rear wall branding (moved, not duplicated)

### Backlighting Method
- Logo areas (icon curves, text) are thinned from 3mm to 1mm
- The surrounding panel stays full 3mm thickness (opaque)
- LED strip is already mounted on the interior wall at Z≈55 (existing channel)
- Light from LED strip hits the thinned areas from behind → soft glow through 1mm PETG
- No additional diffuser layer or hardware needed

### Thinning Detail
- Thinning is from the INSIDE face (not outer) — outer surface stays smooth/flush
- Pocket depth in logo areas: 2mm (3mm panel - 1mm remaining = 2mm pocket on inside)
- Logo pocket edges: 0.5mm draft angle for printability
