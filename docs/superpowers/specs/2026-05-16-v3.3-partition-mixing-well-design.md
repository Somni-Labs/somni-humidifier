# V3.3 Partition-Based Mixing Well — Design Spec

## Summary

Replace the 3-sided box mixing well with a single front/back partition wall spanning the full width of the wet zone. This divides the wet zone into two chambers: a larger front reservoir (60%) and a smaller rear mixing well (40%) containing the atomizer. The atomizer and fill chute swap positions so the atomizer is in the rear (mixing) chamber and the fill chute is in the front (reservoir) chamber.

## Principles

1. **Simple geometry** — one straight wall, not a 3-sided box
2. **Reservoir is bigger** — 60/40 split, reservoir holds bulk clean water
3. **Isolation** — oil only enters the mixing well, never the reservoir
4. **Overflow safety** — notch at top of partition drains excess mixing → reservoir
5. **Minimal constants** — partition defined by Y position + wall thickness + height

## Layout

```
         FRONT (Y = -49.4)
    ┌─────────────────────────────┐
    │                             │
    │      RESERVOIR (60%)        │
    │      fill chute (Y=-15)     │
    │      clean water storage    │
    │                             │
    ├─────────────────────────────┤ ← PARTITION (Y=+10)
    │      overflow notch ↑       │
    │                             │
    │      MIXING WELL (40%)      │
    │      atomizer (Y=+29.7)     │
    │      oil dosing here        │
    │                             │
    └─────────────────────────────┘
         REAR (Y = +49.4)
```

Water pump draws from reservoir (front) → doses into mixing well (rear) over/through partition. Oil pumps dose into mixing well. Atomizer consumes the blend. Overflow notch returns excess to reservoir.

## Geometry

### Partition Wall

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Position (Y center) | +10.0 |
| Wall thickness | 2.5mm |
| Wall height | 15mm (from floor surface) |
| Spans X | Full wet zone width (left outer wall to divider wall) |
| Overflow notch | 3mm wide × full thickness, at top of wall (centered on X) |

### Chamber Dimensions

| Chamber | Y range | Depth | Footprint | Volume at operating depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reservoir (front) | -49.4 to +8.75 | 58.2mm | 39 × 58 = 2290 mm² | ~58ml at 25mm |
| Mixing well (rear) | +11.25 to +49.4 | 38.2mm | 39 × 38 = 1496 mm² | ~7ml at 5mm |

### Moved Positions

| Component | Old Y | New Y | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATOMIZER_POS_Y | 0 | +29.7 | Centered in rear mixing chamber |
| FILL_CHUTE_POS_Y | 18 | -15.0 | Centered in front reservoir chamber |
| MIST_POS_Y | 0 (auto) | +29.7 (auto) | Tracks ATOMIZER_POS_Y |
| EXHAUST_POS_Y | 0 (auto) | +29.7 (auto) | Tracks MIST_POS_Y |

### Clearances

| Check | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Atomizer mount (24mm) to partition | 5.2mm | ✓ |
| Atomizer mount to rear outer wall | 7.7mm | ✓ |
| Fill chute to partition | 25.0mm | ✓ |
| Fill chute to front outer wall | 34.4mm | ✓ |

### Overflow Notch

- 3mm wide notch at top of partition wall
- Centered on X in the wet zone
- If water pump over-doses the mixing well, excess flows over notch back into reservoir
- Height: at 15mm (top of partition wall)

## Scent Change Workflow (unchanged)

1. Stop oil pumps
2. Continue atomizing (burns remaining ~7ml in ~3-4 min)
3. Well runs dry
4. Water pump doses fresh 5-7ml from reservoir into mixing well
5. New oil blend dosed into mixing well
6. Resume atomizing
