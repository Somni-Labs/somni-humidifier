# V3.3 Assembly Channels — Design Spec

## Summary

Add open U-channels and clip guides to the base and top shell so that tubing and wiring route into obvious, separated paths during assembly. Tubes and wires NEVER share a channel. Tubes route along the **divider wall side** (left of center zone). Wires route along the **outer wall side** (right of center zone).

## Principles

1. **Tubes and wires separated** — liquid risk means no shared channels
2. **Shared channels within type** — multiple tubes can share one trough, multiple wires can share one bus
3. **Two tube channels** — left-column (2 tubes) and right-column (3 tubes), matching the pump grid split
4. **Open-top U-shape** — press-fit during assembly, no snapping or threading required
5. **Clip guides for vertical runs** — where gravity would pull tubes/wires away from the wall

## Channel Dimensions

| Channel Type | Width (internal) | Depth | Wall Thickness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-tube trough | 7mm | 4mm | 1.5mm | Holds 2× 3mm tubes side by side |
| 3-tube trough | 10mm | 4mm | 1.5mm | Holds 3× 3mm tubes with slight stack |
| Wire bus | 6mm | 4mm | 1.5mm | Holds 4-5 wire pairs bundled |
| Wire spur | 4mm | 3mm | 1.0mm | Single wire pair to pump |
| Clip guide | 5mm ID | — | 1.5mm | C-shape, 270° wrap, snap-in |

## Tube Channels (Divider Wall Side)

### Outlet Tubes (pump → reservoir)

**Left-column vertical trough** (2 tubes):
- Location: center-zone face of divider wall, X = `DIVIDER_WET_X + WALL_INNER/2 + wall_t + width/2`
- Orientation: vertical, Z from pump-top level (Z=28) down to pass-through holes (Z=24)
- Open toward center zone (open face points +X)
- The 2 left-column pumps are adjacent to the divider — tubes go directly into this trough
- At the bottom, trough aligns with pass-through holes at Y=-13 and Y=+13

**Right-column horizontal bridge** (3 tubes):
- Location: Z=29 (1mm above pump tops), runs X-axis from right-column pump edge to divider
- Open-top U-trough (open face points +Z) 
- Length: ~55mm (from `_pump_right_col_cx` to divider wall)
- Y position: centered at Y=0 (between the Y=-26 and Y=+26 pumps)
- The 3 right-column tubes drop into this bridge from their pump outlets, run horizontally to the divider

**Right-column vertical trough** (3 tubes):
- Location: same divider wall face as left-column trough, but offset in Y
- Actually: combined with left-column trough into one wider section near the divider, since all 5 tubes converge here
- Vertical run from Z=29 (bridge level) down to Z=24 (hole level)
- At the bottom, trough fans out to pass-through holes at Y=-26, Y=0, Y=+26

**Combined divider-face trough:**
- Single vertical U-trough on center-zone face of divider
- Width: 10mm internal (holds 5 tubes stacked/bundled — 3mm OD tubes, 2 across + stagger)
- Depth: 4mm (into the divider wall face, not protruding into center zone)
- This is CUT INTO the divider wall face, not added outward, so it doesn't eat into the 1mm pump clearance
- Runs from Z=29 down to Z=20 (below lowest holes)
- Y span: from Y=-30 to Y=+30 (covers all 5 hole positions)
- All 5 outlet tubes converge here before entering their respective holes
- Open face points +X (toward center zone, easy to press tubes in)

### Inlet Tubes (bottles → pumps)

Inlet tubes drop vertically from the top shell through the open interior. They don't need full channels — just guidance to keep them from tangling.

**Inlet clip guides** (top shell interior, right zone wall):
- 3 clip guides on the inner face of the top shell right-zone wall
- Z positions: evenly spaced between bottle cap level (Z=124) and meeting line (Z=70)
- Each clip holds 2-3 inlet tubes as they descend together
- C-shaped, 270° wrap around tube bundle, open side faces inward for press-fit

**Inlet funnel at base entry:**
- At the base top edge (Z≈65-70), a small flared guide on the divider wall face 
- Guides the 5 inlet tubes from the open shell interior into the center zone pump area
- 15mm wide flared opening tapering to the pump grid area

## Wire Channels (Outer Wall Side)

### Main Wire Bus

- Location: inner face of outer wall (right side), at floor level
- Z: FLOOR_H to FLOOR_H + 4 (channel sits on the floor, open-top)
- Y span: from Y=-35 to Y=+35 (covers all pump positions)
- Open face points -X (toward center zone interior, easy to lay wires in)
- Carries: 5× pump power pairs + LED strip feed + USB-C power

### Pump Power Spurs

- 5 spur channels branching from the main bus inward (-X direction) to each pump
- Each spur: 4mm wide × 3mm deep, runs from outer wall to pump pocket edge
- At floor level (Z = FLOOR_H), cut into the base floor
- Left-column pump spurs are longer (~55mm to cross entire center zone)
- Right-column pump spurs are short (~1mm, pumps are near the outer wall)
- Right-column pumps are right next to the outer wall — their spurs are just 1-2mm stubs from the bus into the pump pocket.
- Left-column pumps are ~55mm from the outer wall. Their spurs run along the floor at Y positions between pump rows (Y≈0, the gap between left-column pumps, and Y≈±20, between right-column pumps). These floor-level spurs pass UNDER the pump pockets (which are cut from FLOOR_H upward) — the spur channels are recessed INTO the floor (Z=0 to Z=FLOOR_H).

### Vertical Wire Riser

- Location: inner face of outer wall, running vertically from floor (Z=3) to tray level (Z=32)
- Position: Y=0 (centered, between pump rows)
- 6mm wide × 4mm deep vertical U-channel on the wall face
- Open face points -X (toward interior)
- Wires rise from the floor bus up to the tray, where they connect to MOSFET/ESP32

### LED Wire Channel

- Branch from the vertical riser at Z≈60 (LED channel height)
- Short horizontal run along the wall to reach the LED strip channel
- 4mm × 3mm spur

## Integration with Existing Features

### Conflicts to Resolve

1. **Pump pockets vs. floor wire spurs**: The left-column pump spur channels run under/between pump pockets. Since pump pockets are cut into the floor starting at FLOOR_H, the spurs need to be at a slightly different Z or routed between pockets (using the Y gaps between pumps).

2. **Tray ledges vs. wire riser**: The tray ledge on the outer wall runs at Z=29-32. The wire riser runs Z=3-32. The riser needs a notch where it passes behind/through the tray ledge, or the ledge needs a gap at Y=0 to let wires through.

3. **Bridge trough vs. tray clearance**: The horizontal tube bridge at Z=29 is only 3mm below the tray (Z=32). The trough walls (1.5mm) + tube (3mm) = 4.5mm — too tall for the 4mm gap. Solution: make the bridge a shallow open groove (2mm deep) with low walls (1mm), relying on the tray bottom above to act as the "lid."

### What Stays the Same

- Pump pocket geometry and shelf ledges
- Divider pass-through holes (5 holes)
- Tray registration tabs and slots
- Atomizer wire chase (already exists, sealed vertical channel)
- Cross-divider wire port (already exists at Z=48)

## Assembly Sequence (Updated)

1. **Route outlet tubes**: press 5 tubes into divider-face trough, thread ends into pass-through holes
2. **Route pump power wires**: lay in outer-wall bus, press spurs toward pump pockets
3. **Drop pumps** into pockets, connect tube from pump output to trough, connect power spur wires
4. **Feed wires up riser** to tray level
5. **Set electronics tray** on ledges
6. **Connect boards** on tray (wires from riser feed into tray edge)
7. **Route inlet tubes** from top shell through clip guides to pump inlets
8. **Place top shell** (magnets align)
