# V3.3 Mixing Well & Water Dosing Pump — Design Spec

## Summary

Add a small rectangular mixing well inside the wet zone, surrounding the atomizer. The well isolates the atomizer from the main reservoir so oil never contaminates the bulk water supply. A 6th dosing pump draws clean water from the reservoir and meters it into the mixing well. The 5 oil pumps dose directly into the well. Scent changes require only burning through ~4.5ml (2-3 minutes) before dosing a new blend.

## Principles

1. **Isolation** — reservoir water stays clean, oil only enters the mixing well
2. **Precision dosing** — both water and oil are pump-controlled, no gravity/valve mixing
3. **Fast scent changes** — tiny volume (~5ml) means the atomizer cycles through it quickly
4. **Overflow safety** — if water pump over-doses, excess drains back to reservoir via notch
5. **Minimal rework** — atomizer stays in same position, divider wall serves as one well wall

## Architecture

```
                RESERVOIR (clean water)
                    │
                    ▼ (6th pump)
    ┌───────────────────────────┐
    │       MIXING WELL         │
    │                           │
    │    oil pumps (5x) ──────► │
    │                           │
    │         ⊙ atomizer        │
    │                           │
    │   overflow ──► reservoir  │
    └───────────────────────────┘
                    │
                    ▼ (mist out)
```

Water pump draws from reservoir → doses into well. Oil pumps dose into well. Atomizer consumes the blend. Overflow lip returns excess to reservoir. Reservoir never gets oil.

## Mixing Well Geometry

### Position & Size

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Inner width (X) | 30mm |
| Inner depth (Y) | 30mm |
| Inner X range | -53.1 to -23.1 |
| Inner Y range | -18.0 to +12.0 |
| Wall thickness | 2.5mm (left, front, rear walls only) |
| Wall height | 15mm (from floor surface) |
| Right wall | Existing divider wall (DIVIDER_WET_X, no new wall) |

### Why Asymmetric Y

- Rear inner edge at Y=+12: leaves 3.5mm gap to fill chute (Y=18) for reservoir fill access
- Front inner edge at Y=-18: atomizer centered at Y=0 has 18mm front clearance (splash room)

### Clearances

| Check | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Left well wall → zone left wall | 6.4mm | ✓ |
| Right → divider wall | Shared (no gap) | ✓ |
| Front well wall → zone front wall | 28.5mm | ✓ |
| Rear well wall → fill chute | 3.5mm | ✓ |
| Atomizer mount → well inner walls | 3.0mm min | ✓ |

### Volumes

| Water depth | Volume |
|---|---|
| 3mm | 2.7ml |
| 5mm (operating) | 4.5ml |
| 8mm | 7.2ml |
| 10mm | 9.0ml |

### Overflow Lip

- 3mm wide notch at top of rear well wall (Y=+12 side)
- Height: at 15mm (top of well wall)
- If water pump over-doses, excess flows over notch back into reservoir
- Reservoir is on the other side (Y > 14.5), receives overflow safely

## 6th Pump (Water Dosing)

### Placement

- Position: Right column, 3rd slot (Y center = 23.9mm)
- Same pump model as oil pumps (JIHPUMP WX3 micro peristaltic)
- Fits with 4.2mm clearance to interior Y max

### Routing

- **Inlet tube:** Draws from reservoir (wet zone, outside the mixing well)
  - Tube exits pump, drops into reservoir area (Y > 14.5, any X in wet zone)
- **Outlet tube:** Doses into mixing well
  - Routes through/over divider wall into mixing well interior
  - Enters well from the right side (divider wall) or over the rear wall

### Wiring

- Same as existing oil pumps — power spur from wire bus
- 6th MOSFET channel on existing MOSFET board (or share enable with water-level logic)

## Oil Pump Tube Routing Change

### Current (incorrect for new design)

Oil pump outlet tubes currently route into the general wet zone (reservoir). With the mixing well, they need to route INTO the well specifically.

### Updated

Oil pump outlet tubes must terminate INSIDE the mixing well (over the well walls or through the divider). The inlet tubes (from bottles) are unchanged.

## Changes to Existing Geometry

### Modify in `build_base()`

1. **Add mixing well walls** — 3 walls (left, front, rear) rising 15mm from floor, centered on atomizer
2. **Add overflow notch** — 3mm wide × full wall thickness cut at top of rear well wall
3. **Move water level sensor** — currently on divider wall inside wet zone; should now monitor RESERVOIR level (outside the mixing well), not well level. Position stays similar but interpret differently.

### Modify in `build_components()`

1. **Add 6th pump** at right column position 3 (Y=23.9)
2. **Route water pump inlet tube** into reservoir area
3. **Route water pump outlet tube** into mixing well
4. **Update oil pump outlet tubes** to terminate inside mixing well (not general reservoir)

## Scent Change Workflow (Software)

1. Stop oil pumps
2. Continue atomizing (burns remaining 4.5ml in ~2-3 min)
3. Well runs dry (or nearly)
4. Water pump doses fresh 4-5ml from reservoir into well
5. New oil blend dosed into well
6. Resume atomizing
